Episode 10

Why Are Flamingos Pink?

Flamingos are born grey. Their pink colour comes from what they eat: tiny shrimp and algae rich in carotenoids, the same pigments that give carrots their colour.

In this episode we travel to the salt marshes of Doñana to see how their diet changes their feathers. Learn more on Wikipedia or listen to more episodes of Why Is That?.
